## State contract

- **Creator Name:** three radio candidates, each with a short rationale.
- **Headline:** one directly editable value.
- **About:** one directly editable multiline value.
- **Search Keywords:** removable tags plus **Add tag**.
- Actions: **Good to go** and **Revise with Weave**.

The MVP must make direct edits and tag addition functional, validate the shared draft schema,
and persist the edited draft. Field-by-field Weave refinement is deferred; do not expose its
action as a dead control. “Good to go” commits exactly the selected/edited values and advances
to the Onboarded state.
