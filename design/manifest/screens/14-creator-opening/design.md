## State contract

Weave explains that a finished business is unnecessary, names the generated outputs (creator
name, headline, About, and search keywords), sets a 5–10 minute expectation, and asks whether
to begin. Actions are a bordered **Yes let’s begin** button and primary-text **I want to do it
later**. “Later” must preserve a resumable structured session; it is not completion.

The visible `+` and microphone are future capabilities. Do not ship them as no-op controls.
